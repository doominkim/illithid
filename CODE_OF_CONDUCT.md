# Code of Conduct

## Community standards

Illithid welcomes participants of all backgrounds, experience levels, and nationalities.
These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces, including people officially representing the project elsewhere.

- Be respectful and considerate. Discuss technical choices without attacking people.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and help others participate.
- Respect differences in language, experience, and accessibility needs.
- Protect other people's privacy. Obtain permission before sharing identifying information or private messages.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized content or unwanted advances, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

The repository maintainer, [@doominkim](https://github.com/doominkim), is responsible for handling conduct concerns.

To arrange a private conversation with the maintainer, [open an issue](https://github.com/doominkim/illithid/issues/new) titled "Private conduct contact request" with only this message:

> I would like a private channel to discuss a community conduct concern.

This contact request is public. Do not include names, incident details, screenshots, private messages, or identifying information until a private channel has been arranged.
If making even a general public contact request would put you at risk, do not use this route.
You can use GitHub's [abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for behavior on GitHub, without posting a public issue.

When reporting privately, include relevant links, a factual account, and any immediate safety concerns.
Reports should be handled discreetly, with details shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
If a concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's reporting process for violations of GitHub's policies.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant for serious or repeated violations.
The response should reflect the severity, context, and impact of the behavior.
When appropriate, the maintainer will explain the decision without exposing confidential report details.
Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.
